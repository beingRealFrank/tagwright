How it works

  1. Prepare. Scanned pages get OCR; missing fonts get embedded.
  2. Tag. Layout analysis builds the structure: headings, paragraphs, lists, tables with header cells, figures, links, and a logical reading order. Page furniture becomes artifacts.
  3. Describe. Each figure gets alt text, and a second, independent check compares it with the image. Disagreements are flagged, not hidden.
  4. Verify. Every output file runs through veraPDF, the open-source PDF/UA validator. Language, title and metadata are set.
  5. Prove. You get an evidence pack: the veraPDF report, a checkpoint table marking each item machine-verified, automated review or human-sampled, an alt-text manifest, a table-header manifest, a reading-order transcript, SHA-256 hashes of input and output, and a signed ledger entry.

Machine checks are necessary, not sufficient. That is why every evidence pack says which checks a machine made, which an automated review made, and which still need a person. Files whose results we cannot stand behind are held back for a manual check instead of being delivered.

Questions

Will this make my organisation compliant?

No tool can promise that, and we don't. Laws such as the ADA, Section 504/508 and the European Accessibility Act depend on your whole service, not one file. What we deliver is a tagged file that passes the machine-checkable parts of PDF/UA-1, plus an honest record of what was and wasn't checked by a person. This is not legal advice.

What happens to my files?

The free check never uploads them. Files you send for fixing are processed by an automated pipeline, are never used to train models, and are deleted 30 days after delivery. Please don't send health records or student records.

Why not just use an auto-tag button?

One-click tagging is cheap but unchecked: you can't see what it guessed. Human remediation shops are thorough but cost $5 to $25 a page. Tagwright sits between: automated, cross-checked, and documented page by page.

Can you fix Word or PowerPoint files?

PDF first. DOCX, PPTX and EPUB are on the way.
