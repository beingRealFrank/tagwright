PDF/UA vs WCAG

Laws cite WCAG. PDF/UA is how you meet it inside a PDF file.

WCAG

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines are the W3C's success criteria for web content. US rules (ADA Title II, Section 508, HHS Section 504) and the European standard EN 301 549 behind the European Accessibility Act point to WCAG 2.1 or 2.2 Level AA. WCAG is technology-neutral: it says what users need (text alternatives, structure, reading order, contrast), not how a PDF must be built.

PDF/UA

PDF/UA (ISO 14289; PDF/UA-1 from 2012, PDF/UA-2 from 2024) is the file-format standard: what a PDF must contain so assistive technology can use it. Tags for every piece of content, artifacts marked, fonts embedded and mapped to Unicode, a language and title, alt text, table headers, and more. The W3C's own PDF techniques for WCAG rely on the same structures.

What to ask for

  • A file that passes veraPDF's PDF/UA-1 profile (the machine-checkable part), with the report.
  • A record of the checks a machine can't make: alt text quality, reading order, heading logic, and who or what judged them.
  • No promise of "compliance". A file can't be compliant with a law on its own; your service is. Vendors who guarantee it are overselling.
