ADA Title II PDF deadline: find your date

Pick your kind of government and its population. You get the date your web content, including every PDF you publish, has to meet WCAG 2.1 AA.




For a state government, use the state population.

Where these dates come from

The Department of Justice's rule under Title II of the ADA sets WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the standard for the web content and mobile apps of state and local governments. On April 20, 2026 the Department published an interim final rule that moved the compliance dates:

  • April 26, 2027 for state and local governments with a total population of 50,000 or more (moved from April 24, 2026);
  • April 26, 2028 for public entities with a population under 50,000, and for any special district government (moved from April 26, 2027).

The technical standard did not change. Title II's general duty not to discriminate is in force today, whatever the rule's dates. Check the Federal Register for anything newer before you plan around these dates.

What it means for your PDFs

The rule covers PDFs and other documents you put on your site. A PDF that meets the standard is tagged, has a logical reading order, marks up headings, lists and tables, describes its images, names its form fields, sets its language and contains real text rather than a picture of text. Some older documents are exempt; our guide to Title II and your PDFs explains which, and how to triage a large library.
