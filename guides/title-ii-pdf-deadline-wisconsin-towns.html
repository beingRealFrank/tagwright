ADA Title II PDF deadline for Wisconsin towns and counties

If your Wisconsin local government serves fewer than 50,000 people you have until April 26, 2028; if it serves 50,000 or more, until April 26, 2027. The PDFs on your site are covered.

The Department of Justice's ADA Title II rule applies to every state and local government in the country, including the Wisconsin governments below. It requires web content, including PDFs, to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. The 2026 interim final rule moved the dates to April 26, 2027 (public entities serving 50,000 people or more) and April 26, 2028 (smaller entities and special district governments). Check the Federal Register for anything newer, and ask your counsel how the rule applies to you; this is not legal advice. Wisconsin may also have its own accessibility requirements, which this page does not cover.

How many Wisconsin governments this affects

The federal .gov registry (CISA's public list of .gov domains, which we use to find sites to scan) lists 784 Wisconsin state, local and special-district domains, including 719 for cities, towns and villages, 65 for counties and 21 for special districts. Not every local government has a .gov domain, so the real number is larger. Most local governments serve fewer than 50,000 people, so the later date applies to most of them. What to do first with your PDFs

  1. Count them. The free checker lists up to 60 PDFs at a time in your browser, with tagged, language, title and scanned status for each, and a CSV.
  2. Cut the pile. In our scan of 33 local government sites only about 1 PDF in 9 was current and untagged, and about a fifth looked old enough to be archive candidates (results). Read what the archived-content exception requires before you move anything.
  3. Fix what residents use: forms and applications and agendas and minutes first.
  4. Ask your peers. The League of Wisconsin Municipalities (website) may have guidance or training for its members.
