Privacy

Free check: runs in your browser. The PDF you choose is read on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers.

Paid remediation: files you send are processed by an automated pipeline. Text and page images may be processed by Anthropic, our processing provider, solely to describe images and check structure; they are not used to train models. Output files and source files are deleted 30 days after delivery. We keep a ledger of SHA-256 hashes and job metadata (never document content) so we can prove what we delivered.

Do not send protected health information or student education records.

Subprocessors: GitHub (website hosting), Netlify (upload storage and file delivery), Polar (payments, as merchant of record), Anthropic (image and text processing for submitted files).

Visit counts

We count page views and free-check runs with a small script that sends only the page name, the event and the referring site's hostname to our own server. No cookies, no IP address and no identifier are stored, and the script does nothing if your browser sends Do Not Track. Page views are also counted by GoatCounter, an open-source analytics service that sets no cookies and stores no personal data.
